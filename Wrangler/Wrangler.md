# The Wrangler

The Wrangler handles the organization and presentation of media after or around preparation.

## Role

The Wrangler owns work such as:

- filenames
- folder names and structure
- titles and years
- metadata cleanup
- track language and title cleanup
- subtitle labeling and selection
- removal or quarantine of unwanted material
- movement into the correct library structure

Encoding and scaling policy belong primarily to [The Cleaner](../Cleaner/Cleaner.md).

## Current Direction

The Wrangler should produce consistent, readable library entries that feel as though they came from one careful release group rather than many unrelated sources.

Established direction includes:

- spaces rather than dotted release names
- stable title and year presentation
- deliberate movie folder rules
- English and English SDH subtitle handling
- removal of unnecessary track titles
- warnings rather than silent assumptions when required information is missing

Exact current behavior should be expanded here from the strongest existing Wrangler source and test record.
